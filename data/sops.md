# Synthetic Facilities SOP Library

> Demo-only sample procedures. These are not DivyaSree internal SOPs and must be replaced with approved site procedures before production use.

## SOP-ELEC-01 — Localised power outage
Confirm the affected area and whether the outage is limited to one circuit or wider. Check for visible hazards before approaching any panel. Only trained personnel may operate electrical panels. Check approved breaker/UPS status and escalate if repeated tripping occurs. Record the circuit, time, observed condition, and action taken.

## SOP-ELEC-02 — Electrical safety escalation
Smoke, sparks, exposed live conductors, electric shock, or water near live electrical equipment require immediate safety escalation. Keep untrained personnel away. Do not instruct a user to touch, reset, or inspect exposed electrical parts. Notify the designated on-duty safety/facilities contact.

## SOP-HVAC-01 — Comfort complaint
Confirm the affected zone, measured temperature if available, time of onset, and whether neighbouring zones are affected. Check thermostat/BMS status, airflow, and obvious unit alarms. If multiple zones are affected, escalate to HVAC/MEP for central-system checks.

## SOP-PLUMB-01 — Water leak
Locate the apparent source and identify whether electrical systems or occupied areas are at risk. Contain water using approved methods. Only trained staff should isolate valves. Photograph/record the source and affected area. Escalate immediately if water is near electrical equipment.

## SOP-LIFT-01 — Lift fault
Confirm lift ID, location, and whether occupants are trapped. Entrapment is an emergency escalation; do not attempt rescue unless trained and authorised. For non-entrapment faults, take the lift out of service if required by site procedure and contact the approved lift OEM/service team.

## SOP-SEC-01 — Access control issue
Verify the user identity through approved process before changing access. Check whether the issue is badge-specific, reader-specific, or site-wide. Never bypass security controls for convenience. Escalate suspicious or repeated failed access events to the security lead.

## SOP-HK-01 — Spill / housekeeping incident
Secure the area to prevent slips or exposure. Identify the spill type before cleanup. Use approved PPE and disposal method. If the substance is unknown or hazardous, escalate rather than proceeding with routine cleanup.

## SOP-IT-01 — Network / AV incident
Confirm the affected device, room, and number of users. Check power, cabling, network link, and approved service status. Avoid factory resets unless configuration is backed up and authorised. Record steps already attempted before escalating.

## SOP-CIVIL-01 — Building fabric issue
Inspect the reported door, ceiling, floor, wall, or fixture. Make the area safe if there is a falling-object or trip risk. Capture dimensions and photos for repair planning. Structural movement, falling debris, or collapse indicators require immediate escalation.
